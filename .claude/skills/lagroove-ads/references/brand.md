# Lagroove — Brand Facts, Voice & Copy Bank

## Facts (keep accurate)

- **Name:** Lagroove (one word; wordmark is the groovy serif logo).
- **What:** Boutique **Lagree / Megaformer Pilates** studio. The workout is
  Lagree — slow, controlled, high-time-under-tension movement on a Megaformer.
  Positioned explicitly as ***not*** regular Pilates: *"It's not Pilates, it's
  Lagree."*
- **Location:** 1657 28th St, Boulder, CO.
- **Taglines:** *"Boulder's grooviest fitness studio."* and the logo-stamp
  tagline *"Tune In · Tone Up."*
- **Web:** `www.LAGROOVE.com` · Handle: `@lagroove`.
- **Verify before stating** anything specific not listed here (prices, exact
  class times, instructor names, current promos) — those change. The reference
  graphics show *examples* (e.g. "Saturdays @ 10am") that may be outdated.

## Voice

Warm, playful, body-positive, lightly cheeky. Disco-joyful but grounded in real
fitness coaching. Talks *with* members, not *at* them. Puns on Lagree/groove are
on-brand and encouraged. Never corporate, clinical, or hustle-grindy. Inclusive
and encouraging about bodies and beginners.

**Signature move:** the Lagree/groove/agree wordplay — "Let's Lagree," "Lagroove,"
"the groove." And reframing Lagree as distinct from Pilates.

**Lagree education angles**
- Lagree is all about *time under tension* — slow, controlled movement that
  brings on the shake and the burn.
- Benefits: lean sculpted definition · core stability (great for moms-to-be &
  new moms) · metabolism boost · joint-friendly · mental reset · bigger booty.
- Move names get playful headlines: "Master your Catfish," "Which move humbles
  you most?"
- "Form Focus" style classes: beginner-friendly + experienced; two instructors,
  extra hands-on adjustments.

**Naming/quirks**
- "Catfish," and other Lagree move names, are used affectionately as headlines.
- Holiday/event posts lean into the disco-ball motif ("The Disco Ball Draw,"
  NYE disco balls).

## Do / Don't

**Do:** stay green; lead with disco-ball or sunburst motifs; keep it warm and
punny; leave breathing room; sign off with the wordmark or lagroove.com; tint
photos green when compositing into colored grounds.

**Don't:** introduce off-brand colors (blue/red/etc.) beyond tiny realistic photo
accents; clutter the layout; write corporate/grindy copy; call the workout
"Pilates"; stack three decorative fonts; invent specific prices/times/promos —
ask or mark them as placeholders.
