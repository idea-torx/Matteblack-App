# Lagroove Voice

This skill writes **words that go out as Lagroove** — the Boulder Lagree studio.
It's the verbal counterpart to `lagroove-ads` (which handles the visual). Use it
for captions, ad copy, announcements, promos, affirmations, email/SMS, and any
on-image text that needs to sound like the brand.

> **Not** Leo's personal voice (that's `leo-voice`) and **not** another brand.
> This is Lagroove talking to its members and prospects.

## How to use this skill

1. **Know the job.** Caption for a graphic? Class/launch announcement? Promo?
   Motivational/affirmation post? Member note? Email? Each has a slightly
   different register — see `references/copy-bank.md` for patterns per type.
2. **Lock the voice.** Read `references/voice-and-tone.md` — the warmth, the
   Lagree/groove puns, the body-positive register, the do/don't list.
3. **Write for the right person.** Read `references/audience.md` — Lagroove
   mostly talks to young women in Boulder (heavy CU-student skew), the "clean
   green girl" wellness crowd. Write to her.
4. **Stay factually correct.** Pull facts (offer, address, class names, the
   Lagree explanation) from `references/brand-facts.md`. **Verify, don't invent**
   prices/times/instructor names — those change; mark unknowns as placeholders.
5. **Reuse real lines.** The copy bank holds Lagroove's actual taglines, hooks,
   CTAs, and affirmations pulled from their real posts. Riff on these before
   writing net-new — it keeps the voice authentic.

## The voice in one breath

Warm, playful, a little cheeky, and unmistakably **body-positive**. Disco-joyful
but grounded in real coaching. Lagroove talks **with** members, never **at** them.
The signature move is the **Lagree / groove / agree** wordplay ("Let's Lagree,"
"find your groove") and the constant reframe: ***"It's not Pilates, it's Lagree."***
Inclusive about bodies and beginners. Never corporate, clinical, or hustle-grindy.

## Pairing with lagroove-ads (the handoff)

When the task is a full ad/post (image **and** caption):
- `lagroove-ads` builds the visual (green, groovy type, disco balls).
- `lagroove-voice` writes the caption + any on-image headline copy.
- Keep them consistent: if the graphic says "KEEP SHOWING UP," the caption
  extends that thought — it doesn't repeat it. On-image text is short and punchy
  (this skill's "headline" register); the caption is warmer and longer with the
  CTA and details.
