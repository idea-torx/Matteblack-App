## The workout (explain it right)

- **Lagree**, performed on a **Megaformer**. **High-intensity, low-impact.**
- **~50–55 minute** classes. Slow, controlled, high-**time-under-tension**
  movement → "the shake" and "the burn."
- Benefits: tightens & tones the whole body, lean/sculpted definition, core
  stability, joint-friendly, minimizes injury risk.
- **"Designed for all"** — expecting parents, Olympic athletes, feel-good
  enthusiasts; beginners through advanced.
- Studio experience: bright, clean, retro-chic; Megaformers under a **disco
  ball**; a **legendary sound system**; strong sense of **community** ("second
  home").

## The reframe (use constantly)

> **"It's not Pilates, it's Lagree."**

Lagree is its own method — faster-failing, higher-intensity, Megaformer-based —
not a Pilates class. The whole brand hinges on this distinction. Never call a
Lagroove class "Pilates."
