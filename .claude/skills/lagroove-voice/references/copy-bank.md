## Pun & wordplay bank (lean in, this is the brand)

The name is a triple pun: **La**gree + **groove** + ag**ree**. Mine all three.
"Lagroove" also works as a **verb** ("come Lagroove with us"). House rule: one
*great* pun per piece beats three forced ones. ⭐ = house favorites.

**The reframe riffs** (canonical line + playful variants)
- ⭐ "It's not Pilates, it's Lagree." (the anchor, keep using it)
- ⭐ "It's not Pilates, it's Lagroove 😉" (the cheeky house riff)
- "Sorry Pilates, we Lagree to disagree."
- "Pilates walked so Lagree could groove."
