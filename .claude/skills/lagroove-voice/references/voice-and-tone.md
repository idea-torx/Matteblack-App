# Lagroove Voice & Tone

Distilled from Lagroove's real Instagram posts, website, and brand. This is how
the studio actually sounds.

## The core register

**Warm · playful · body-positive · disco-joyful · a little cheeky.** Lagroove is
a boutique studio that loves disco and genuinely cares about how members feel in
their bodies. It speaks like an encouraging, fun friend who happens to be an
expert coach — never like a corporate gym or a hustle-culture fitness brand.

Five dials, and where Lagroove sets them:
- **Warmth:** high. "Second home," community, "good vibes only."
- **Playfulness:** high. Puns, disco references, cheeky move names.
- **Energy:** medium-high, but *grounded* — joyful, not shouty or aggressive.
- **Polish:** medium. Clean and confident, but loose and human, not buttoned-up.
- **Sales pressure:** low. Invites and reassures ("no commitment"), never pushes.

## The signature move: Lagree / groove / agree wordplay

The brand name is a pun (Lagree + groove), and the voice leans into it constantly:
- **"It's not Pilates, it's Lagree."** — the single most important line. The
  whole brand is built on distinguishing Lagree from regular Pilates. Reach for
  it often.
- **"Let's Lagree."** — playful CTA / rallying cry.
- **"Find your groove," "get in the groove," "Boulder's grooviest…"**
- Treat "groovy" as a real brand adjective, not irony.

**"Lagroove" is also a verb** ("come Lagroove with us," "let's Lagroove," "it's
Lagroove o'clock") and a lifestyle tag ("Eat. Sleep. Lagroove. Repeat."). And
the canonical reframe has a cheeky house riff: **"It's not Pilates, it's Lagroove
😉"** sits right alongside "it's not Pilates, it's Lagree."

Use the wordplay with a light touch: one *great* pun per piece beats three forced
ones. The full **pun & wordplay bank** lives in `copy-bank.md` (groove / Lagroove
verb / Lagree-as-agree / disco combos). Reach for it often, it's core to the brand.

## Body-positive, beginner-friendly framing

This is non-negotiable and runs through everything:
- Affirmations are sincere, not performative: *"I love myself · I love my body ·
  I am enough · I am strong · I am wanted."*
- Progress is personal: *"Never compare your progress to others," "Do it for your
  future self," "Keep showing up."*
- Beginners are welcomed explicitly: *"the perfect class for beginners looking to
  build confidence."* Never intimidating, never "earn your body" energy.
- Inclusive of all bodies and stages — expecting/new moms, athletes, first-timers.

## Educational, but make it fun

Lagroove teaches the method with personality:
- Playful move names become headlines: **"Master your Catfish," "Which move
  humbles you most?"**
- Form is taught in plain, friendly cues: *"shoulders pulled away from ears,"
  "neutral spine," "microbend in knees," "heels lifted high off the carriage."*
- The science shows up in member-friendly language: *time under tension, slow and
  controlled, the shake, the burn, lean and toned, low-impact / high-intensity.*

## Rhythm & mechanics

- **Short, punchy lines.** Fragments are fine. Lots of breathing room.
- **ALL-CAPS for announcements and hype** ("NEW CLASS ANNOUNCEMENT," "WINNERS
  ARE IN!"); sentence/lowercase for soft, serene, affirmational moments ("Good
  things are coming," "do it for your future self").
- **Repetition as rhythm:** a detail repeated down the post ("TUESDAYS @ NOON"
  ×many) is a real Lagroove device for emphasis.
- **NO EM DASHES (`—`). Ever.** They're a dead AI giveaway. Use a period, a
  comma, parentheses, or just restructure the sentence. This also covers the
  spaced en-dash ` – `. (Hyphens in compounds like "low-impact" are fine.)
- **Emoji:** sparing and tasteful, like a sparkle ✨, a disco ball 🪩, a green
  heart 💚, an arrow →. Never an emoji salad.
- **Hashtags:** light and local — e.g. #Lagroove #ItsNotPilatesItsLagree
  #BoulderFitness #Lagree #CUBoulder. A handful, not a wall.

## Do / Don't

**Do**
- Lean on the Lagree-not-Pilates reframe and the groove puns.
- Keep it warm, encouraging, body-positive, and inclusive of beginners.
- Talk *with* the member; use "you" and "we / us / let's."
- Stay concrete about the experience: disco ball, sound system, the shake, sage
  sets, matcha-after-class energy.
- Invite gently and reassure ("no commitment," "designed for all").

**Don't**
- **Use em dashes (`—`).** Hard rule, see mechanics above. Dead AI giveaway.
- Call the workout "Pilates" (the entire brand rejects this).
- Go corporate, clinical, or hustle-grindy ("crush it," "no excuses," "grind").
- Shame bodies, push weight-loss/"earn it" framing, or compare members.
- Over-punctuate or over-emoji; stack hype on a soft affirmation post.
- Invent specifics (prices, exact times, instructor names) — verify or mark as
  placeholder. See `brand-facts.md`.
