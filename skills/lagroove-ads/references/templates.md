# Lagroove Ad Templates

The recurring layouts in the Lagroove feed. Match the message to a template, open
its exemplar in `assets/reference-images/`, then build (Mode B) or write a gen
prompt (Mode A). "Hybrid" = generate the background (A), set type on top (B).

Quick chooser:

| If the ad's job is… | Use template |
|---|---|
| Announce/launch a class, schedule, event (with details) | **Info-card-over-photo**, **Paper poster + line icon**, **Duotone headline poster** |
| Big bold hype / brand statement | **Repeated headline**, **Engagement question**, **Layered type + neon** |
| Motivation / quote / affirmation | **Aura-gradient quote**, **Rings quote**, **Wavy affirmations**, **Memo card**, **Groovy type + flowers** |
| Educate (form, benefits, how-to) | **Form-cue diagram**, **Scrapbook benefits collage**, **Carousel cover** |
| Vibe / mood / community / repost | **Psychedelic swirl**, **Polaroid on texture**, **Vintage halftone collage**, **Moodboard grid**, **Dark disco** |
| Merch / sticker / playful slogan | **Vintage merch print** |

## Announcement / details

### Info-card-over-photo  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-info-card-over-photo_form-focus*
Rounded sage card floats over a blurred green-toned class photo. Card holds:
wordmark, condensed-serif headline (sometimes with a script word overlaid),
short body with **underlined** emphasis, a bold schedule line, and
`BOOK NOW AT www.LAGROOVE.com`. **The workhorse for any class/offer with real
details.** Hybrid: gen the blurred photo bg, build the card in code.

### Paper poster + line icon  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-paper-poster-lineicon_new-class-times*
Minty crumpled-paper ground, black type, a single thin line-art icon (disco
ball) centered. Masthead corners (month / LAGROOVE), display-serif headline,
script subhead, ALL-CAPS sans details. Clean and editorial. Mode B (icon as SVG).

### Duotone headline poster  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-duotone-headline-poster_megaphone*
Solid sage ground, monumental headline, a single green-**duotone** cutout photo
object (megaphone+flowers, etc.), a repeated-line text column for rhythm, address
masthead, `MORE INFO >`. Hybrid.

## Hype / statement

### Repeated headline  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-repeated-headline-cutout_lets-lagree*
The headline stacked and repeated down the frame (e.g. "LET'S LAGREE ×5") in big
condensed sans, with cutout model photos (on reformers) layered in front. Torn-
paper edge optional. High energy. Hybrid (gen cutouts, build the type wall).

### Engagement question  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-engagement-question-cutout_which-move*
Cream ground, enormous condensed-sans question in olive ("WHICH MOVE GETS YOU
EVERY TIME?"), green body copy, a cutout model holding a disco ball, wordmark
footer. Ends with a "swipe / comment" prompt. Hybrid.

### Layered type + neon script  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-layered-type-neon-script_motivation*
Dark-olive ground. A big spaced sans word (e.g. "MOTIVATION") with a glowing
lime **neon script** phrase ("be your own") overlaid across it. Mode B.

## Motivation / quote

### Aura-gradient quote  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-aura-gradient-quote_good-things-coming*
Soft radial green aura glow, a single small centered line in clean serif. Serene,
"spiritual wellness." Mode B (CSS radial-gradient).

### Aura glow + doodle  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-aura-glow-doodle_grateful*
Same glow energy but playful: marker-script lead-in inside a hand-drawn circle
with a curvy arrow pointing to a big serif word ("grateful."), wordmark footer.
Mode B.

### Rings quote  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-rings-quote_future-self*
Solid green ground with thin concentric hairline rings, an italic clean-serif
line centered ("Do it for your future self"). Mode B.

### Wavy affirmations  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-wavy-affirmations_i-love-myself*
Full-bleed stack of affirmations in **groovy wavy bubble** type, tonal green on
cream ("I LOVE MYSELF / I AM ENOUGH…"). Mode B (`Bagel Fat One`).

### Memo card  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-memo-card_never-compare*
A faux "MEMO" form (DATE / TO: Members) as a card over a disco-ball model photo,
with a marker-script message inside. Personable, like a note to members. Hybrid.

### Groovy type + flowers  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-groovy-type-flowers_keep-showing-up*
Cream paper, a mixed-type slogan (groovy display + bubble script, "KEEP Showing
UP"), retro daisies and a halftone disco ball, `www.LAGROOVE.com`. Mode B + one
image asset.

## Educational

### Form-cue diagram  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-form-cue-diagram_master-catfish*
Painterly green ground, condensed-sans headline naming a Lagree move ("MASTER
YOUR CATFISH"), a cutout model on the Megaformer, white arrows + sans labels
pointing to each form cue. Great for teaching technique. Hybrid.

### Scrapbook benefits collage  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-scrapbook-benefits-collage*
Sage ground, marker-script "BENEFITS OF LAGREE", a cutout model in the center
with hand-drawn arrows + script labels radiating out, a taped "polaroid" of the
Megaformer, doodle hearts/sparkles, disco ball. Warm and editorial. Hybrid.

### Carousel cover (duotone)  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-carousel-cover-duotone_first-class*
Full-bleed green-duotone class photo, huge white display-serif headline ("HOW TO
PREP FOR YOUR FIRST LAGREE CLASS"), a `swipe →` pill. Opens educational
carousels. Hybrid.

## Vibe / mood / repost

### Psychedelic swirl  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-psychedelic-swirl-circular-text_not-pilates*
70s tonal-green liquid-swirl ground. Either **circular/arched text** around a
center sunburst ("IT'S NOT PILATES IT'S LAGREE…") or a screenshot-style tweet/
meme card laid on top. Playful. Mode B (or hybrid for the swirl texture).

### Polaroid on texture  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-polaroid-clipped-texture_good-vibes*
Green croc-leather (or other texture) ground, a B&W or color polaroid clipped
with a binder clip, a script caption beneath, wordmark. Tactile. Hybrid.

### Vintage halftone collage  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-vintage-halftone-collage_hand-disco*
Retro cut-paper / halftone collage — e.g. a hand dangling a mirror-ball — flat
green ground, gritty print texture, a sparkle, small realistic accents (red
nails). Strong hero image. Mode A.

### Moodboard grid  ·  *exemplar: moodboard-grid_green-aesthetic*
3×3 grid of on-palette green lifestyle shots (matcha, sage activewear, mint car,
palms, satin, swirl). Pure vibe / brand DNA. Mode A (curate or generate tiles).

### Dark disco  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-dark-disco_nye*
Black ground, glowing green photographic disco balls with lens sparkle,
condensed-serif headline, doodle circle on a key detail. For night/party/holiday
moments (NYE). Hybrid.

### Scalloped-frame wordmark  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-scalloped-frame-wordmark_disco-ball-draw*
Two-tone green with a wavy **scalloped border** framing the content, wordmark +
small-caps serif + signature script + a white line-art disco ball. For
contests/special features. Mode B.

### Silk-texture disco  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-silk-texture-disco_winners*
Draped green satin ground, wordmark, script + condensed-serif headline, hanging
checkered disco balls, sparkles, "Swipe →". Premium event feel. Hybrid.

## Merch / playful

### Vintage merch print  ·  *exemplar: tmpl-vintage-merch_direction-over-speed*
Single-color screenprint look on natural/cream stock — groovy display type +
a simple line-art creature/icon + a wholesome slogan ("Direction over Speed /
Enjoy the Journey"). Reads like a tote/tee graphic. Mode B (or A for the
illustration). Note: this one runs warmer (forest green on natural canvas) and
is the most "merch," least "studio-ad" of the set.

## House art-direction (applies to every photo)

Models in **sage/lime activewear** (unitards, sets), bright clean studio or the
Lagroove space, often on black Megaformers, white grip socks, gold jewelry,
sometimes interacting with a **mirror-ball prop**. Lifestyle shots lean "clean
green girl": matcha, tote bags, neutral skin tones, natural light. See
`art-direction_*` exemplars and `references/generation-prompts.md`.
